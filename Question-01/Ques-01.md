## Exercise 1: The "Profile Card" (Variables & Data Types)

### Goal
Practice declaring variables and checking types.

### Instructions
- Create a `const` variable named `fullName` and assign your name to it.
- Create a `let` variable named `age` and assign your age.
- Create a `let` variable named `isStudent` and assign a Boolean value (`true` or `false`).

### Tasks
- Change the `age` variable to a new number (simulate a birthday!).
- Console log a sentence that says:  
  "My name is [Name], I am [Age] years old, and it is [true/false] that I am a student."

### Bonus
- Console log the `typeof` your `isStudent` variable.
